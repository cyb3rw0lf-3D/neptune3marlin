Training Center

Service Bay VR

Learn real automotive service procedures in a virtual shop: oil changes, tire service and more, guided by Valkyrie the shop drone.

Controls
  • VR controllers: point and pull the trigger to use or click. Grip grabs a tool or puts it back. Twist your wrist while holding the trigger to turn wrenches. Push the left stick forward to aim a teleport and let go to jump. Right stick snap turns.
  • Hands: pinch to click, grab and use. Point at the blue floor rings to move.
  • Mouse: click to grab or use, and hold the click for wrench and hold jobs. Drag empty space to look, WASD to walk, and click the blue floor rings to jump to work spots. Mouse wheel sets the torque wrench. Q puts a tool back, H gives a hint in Practice, Esc pauses.
  • Touch: tap to use, drag to look, tap the blue rings to move.
